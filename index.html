Adidas & The World Cup

A Legacy Since 1954

Adidas and the FIFA World Cup share a partnership that spans over seven decades. The relationship began in 1954 when the German national team, wearing Adidas boots with revolutionary screw-in studs, won the World Cup in Switzerland—an event known as the "Miracle of Bern."

The Official Match Ball

Since 1970, Adidas has been the official supplier of match balls for every FIFA World Cup. The Telstar, introduced in Mexico 1970, became iconic with its black and white panels designed for visibility on television. Each tournament since has featured innovative ball designs—from the Tango (1978) to the Brazuca (2014) and Al Rihla (2022).

Iconic Moments

Countless World Cup legends have worn the three stripes: Franz Beckenbauer, Michel Platini, Zinedine Zidane, and Lionel Messi. The brand has been present for historic moments—from Maradona's "Hand of God" to Zidane's headbutt, and Messi's crowning glory in Qatar 2022.

Innovation & Technology

Adidas continues to push boundaries in football technology. From Predator boots that revolutionized ball control to the latest connected ball technology that assists VAR decisions, Adidas remains at the forefront of football innovation on the world's biggest stage.

2026 & Beyond

As the World Cup expands to 48 teams and heads to North America in 2026, Adidas continues its commitment to the beautiful game—outfitting national teams, providing match balls, and creating the eyewear collections that let fans see every moment clearly.
